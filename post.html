Finally got my compiler to survive a full self-hosted build

Three weeks of chasing a segfault that only happened on Tuesdays. Turned out to be an uninitialized struct field in the symbol table that only got read on the second pass of a self-hosted compile.

Writing up the full debugging story on my blog later this week — the short version is: always zero your structs, and gdb watchpoints are worth the setup time.
