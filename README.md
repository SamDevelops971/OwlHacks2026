# TUgether 🦉

### OwlHacks 2026

**TUgether** is a student-built platform designed to make it easier for Temple University students to discover and connect with the resources available across campus.

Temple offers a wide range of **clubs, organizations, faculty, events, and research opportunities**, but finding the right information can sometimes be difficult. TUgether brings these resources together in one convenient and manageable website.

## 🎯 Our Goal

We wanted to create a centralized platform where Temple students can easily:

* 🔎 Discover student clubs and organizations
* 📅 Find upcoming campus events
* 👩‍🏫 Explore Temple faculty and their areas of expertise
* 🔬 Find research opportunities
* 🏫 Learn more about resources and facilities available at Temple
* 🤝 Connect with opportunities and communities that match their interests

## 💡 Why TUgether?

There are countless opportunities available to Temple students, but information can be spread across different websites, departments, social media pages, and campus resources.

**TUgether brings these opportunities together in one place.**

Instead of searching through multiple sources, students can use TUgether as a starting point to discover what's happening around Temple and find opportunities that interest them.
